Merchant Monthly Pricing

Set recurring monthly fees per Merchant with the exact date they should be recognised. Add a new effective date whenever you want to increase, reduce or stop a fee; older records remain unchanged.

Monthly fee is recognised on the selected charge date, then on the same day each following month.
Example: TITANX MYR 2,000 with charge date 07 Sep 2026 posts MYR 2,000 on 07 Sep, 07 Oct, 07 Nov, etc. To change the price, add a new effective date and amount. Set amount to 0.00 from a selected date to stop future charges.
